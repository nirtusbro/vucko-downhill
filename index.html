Find your flow.

Drag left or right with your thumb to carve. Sharper carving costs speed, so keep your turns smooth.

Pass between the matching flags.
Link gates for up to a ×8 combo, and thread the middle for a +50 bullseye. The gates tighten and the slope steepens toward the finish.

Every level holds five lamps. Four wait off the racing line, each with a rock on the line beside it: go wide for the lamp or cut inside past the rock. Clear every gate and reach the goal score to earn the fifth and unlock the next level.
Twenty levels, a hundred lamps.

Now and then a present falls from the sky!
Follow the gold ring and collect it after it lands for +200 points.

Levels get faster and tighter, with more rocks and trees. A tumble costs 300 points, your speed and your combo, and a missed gate means no finish lamp this run. Lamps you pick up are saved at once, even if you leave the run early.
Once you have a best run on a level, a pale Vučko races it beside you and the timer shows how far ahead or behind you are.

Finish faster for more points: +50 for every second under 90 seconds, awarded at the finish.

Miss a gate? Keep going. Take a tumble? Vučko gets right back up.

On a keyboard, use A / D or ← / →.
